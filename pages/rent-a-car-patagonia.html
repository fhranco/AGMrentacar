Common questions about renting a car in Patagonia

check_circle Where can I pick up my rental car in Patagonia?

You can pick up your vehicle at Punta Arenas city or directly at Presidente Carlos Ibáñez del Campo International Airport (PUQ). Pickup is coordinated with your flight arrival time.

check_circle Does the rental include unlimited mileage?

Yes. All AGM vehicles come with unlimited mileage, so you can drive freely across Patagonia without worrying about extra charges per kilometre.

check_circle Can I drive to Torres del Paine with the rental car?

Yes. Our SUV and 4x4 fleet is suitable for the gravel roads leading to Torres del Paine National Park, approximately 3 hours from Punta Arenas.

check_circle Can I cross the border into Argentina?

Yes. Border crossing to Argentina (Ushuaia, El Calafate, etc.) is possible with prior notice when requesting your quote. We handle the notarial authorization and mandatory binational insurance.
